# Simple Tower social counterpart boundary

**Decision:** Jay, 2026-11-03. Rosa Brandl and Mr. McFeely are deliberately simpler than the principal simulated inhabitant, Septimus Pretorius.

Pretorius is the sole fully modeled person. The social counterparts supply encounters, unequal cadence, physical handoffs, customs, limited witnessed knowledge, and credible constraints. Their value is as persistent sources of testable social cause and information exchange. They are not miniature versions of Pretorius.

**Rosa Brandl** is a scripted merchant with a thirty-year reconstructed archive prior from E22-001 and E22-003, a physical supply/account workflow, established measurements and floor safety customs, context-triggered noticings, and separate finite event history. Her reactions are structured, not generated by a second model or hidden cognitive controller. The archive prior diminishes when contradicted or supplemented by lived encounters.

**Mr. McFeely** is a scripted postal carrier with a distinctive triple knock, two or three scheduled rounds a week, path-only presence, physical mail/parcel exchange, and a small stock of sourced claims. His knowledge changes only through witnessed events, carried correspondence, or declared source chains. He never enters the tower or develops a separate simulated inner life.

**Test boundary:** relationship treatment in Pretorius must derive from witnessed and received event histories, not branch on scripted actor ID. The swap control must continue to work. Sender claims remain claims, no correspondence can invent or append a canonical archive memory. If future changes require speech variety, prefer bounded scripts using existing sourced facts over independent simulated minds. No second model admission or autonomous companion layer is authorized.

**Rationale:** giving the supporting characters the same cognitive architecture would confound the Articles 05/06 experiment, add implementation cost, and shift attention from the question under test: whether a single situated artificial inhabitant can form differentiated relationships and multi-party beliefs in a persistent life.
