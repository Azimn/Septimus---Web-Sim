# Preregistered folio influence experiment v1

**Frozen before any outcome comparisons, November 1, 2026.** Preserve protocol and null results. The frozen 450-memory v12 file must not be modified. No LLM calls.

Question: after Septimus naturally chooses to reach and physically read a specific historical journal folio, does that event alter his subsequent **selected goals**, compared with the same simulation without access to the folio or with a contrary substituted control folio?

Three source anchors and prespecified predicted actions: **E22-001** (Rosa Brandl's cart and unsafe stair floor, 1869) -> `inspect_floor`; **E18-010** (nettles in the tower garden, 1898) -> `tend_path`; **E03-002** (late/wet coal harms heat, 1868) -> `order_coal`. These are authored reconstructed memories, not lived events newly created in the simulation.

Design: 24 seeds, numbered 1 through 24, for each of three folios. Matched arms share the exact initial scenario configuration and identical engine, physical world, weather, and seed. The harness may preset the *next journal folio index*, not prescribe a goal or action. Wait at most **800 simulated ticks** for an **endogenously selected** `read_journal` goal to reach the notebook. No event means a no-trigger, not success. After a common physical read event, A (read) exposes the source-verified original folio and applies a policy score effect of **+18** to the predicted action; B (withheld) records the read attempt but blocks access, applying zero; C (replaced) uses a same-topic contrary fictional counter-folio, explicitly labeled `experiment_control_substitution` in every experimental record, applying **-18**. Controls exist **only** inside the experimental harness, not in `canon/`, identity, ordinary character memories or continued game saves. A/B/C score perturbations are engineered controller interventions, **not semantic reading comprehension**.

After the trigger run **160 additional ticks**. Primary outcome: fraction of **all 24 matched seeds** whose first five subsequent **`goal_selected` decision sequences** differ between A and B. Secondary outcomes: target action chosen at least once within that window, and A/C first-five divergence. No-trigger seeds count as unsuccessful in the 24-seed denominator and are individually recorded. Predefined source-level criterion: at least **30% of all seeds** have A/B divergence *and* A increases the target-selection rate relative to B. Overall label 'load-bearing in the simple policy' requires **at least two of three** tested folios to satisfy both criteria. If not, report **null** unchanged, without changing thresholds or retuning afterward. Every seed/arm includes selected-goal sequences and provenance references. Results must be checked into `results/folio_experiment_v1.json` and `results/folio_experiment_v1.md`. Preserve failures and no-trigger runs.

All comparisons occur in disposable state only; the living world's journal, file history, canonical archive and physics never change as a consequence of experimental control substitutions.
