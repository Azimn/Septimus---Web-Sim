# Home continuity with the 450-memory reconstructed autobiography

Septimus lives at **the ruined tower outside Ingolstadt, Bavaria**, not at Ash Grove. This is a descriptive label, not a proper name. This game is the **pre-Frankenstein Village** era of the same fictional Dr. Septimus Pretorius.

Primary source: https://github.com/Azimn/Pretorius-Connectome/blob/main/memories/current/Pretorius_v12_450_Events_Complete.jsonl

Other attested spaces: study, library, laboratory, glass chambers, archive, pantry, music room, sleeping chamber, photography room, roof terrace and stairwell. Not all are guaranteed to be present at the same time.

**Time setting:** after the June 1899 memory and before the separate future Frankenstein Village game. No source supplies a date for a village relocation.

**Gameplay inventions, NOT historical evidence:** the flat 16 x 10 tile map, unlimited household water service, washroom arrangement, tea cultivation, garden allotments, toy books, catalog prices and starting inventory. These mechanics may exist within this fictional simulation but may not be added retroactively to the frozen autobiography. World receipts and reconstructed memories remain separately attributed.

The legacy Ash Grove identity was not silently overwritten in existing saves. One allowlisted, hash-linked `CANON_CORRECTION` event records the external identity-label correction without changing any previous world state, receipts or memories. Unapproved identity edits remain prohibited.

Future features with strong memory support: leaky roof, coal prices and deliveries, faulty heating, floor/stair repairs, room-capacity constraints, external suppliers and visitor correspondence. The eventual move to Frankenstein Village requires its own explicit provenance-preserving transition; it is not a pre-scripted past event.
