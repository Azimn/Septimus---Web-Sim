# Tower household conditions v1 | verified autobiographical anchors

**Implemented on the independent Septimus map.** This remains a modest deterministic game simulation, not a reconstructed building survey, authentic nineteenth-century economy, or new cognitive architecture. The source-backed history is in `docs/HOME_CONTINUITY_AUDIT.md`; the character's real in-game observations and each fictional old folio retain separate provenance.

**Coal, cart, and warmth (E03-002, E22-012, E22-001):** Coal is a separate coal-pile stock at the porch, not pantry food. Orders are paid from the existing wallet via the catalog, then arrive by Rosa Brandl's firm after an explicitly seeded 1 to 9 extra ticks (plus standard 24 transit ticks). Wetness is deterministic and may reduce heating duration from 26 to 12 ticks. Coal dries on some non-rainy days. The cart arrival is a world event; it does not create Rosa as a visiting NPC or first-person memory when he is absent. The pile is explicitly counted during an `inspect_coal` visit. The kitchen hearth consumes an actual dry or wet basket, and can warm linked rooms through simplified heating pipes. The date's winter factor raises some grocery, coal and scientific catalog prices. Coal and food and research consumables spend the same wallet. This pressure is observable in expenses, not narrative assertions.

**Tower fabric (E03-001, E22-001, E03-020, E22-012):** A weak floor and worn roof have continuous integrity. Stairs degrade slowly with traffic and can cause harm at severe disrepair. Rain speeds roof deterioration. When the roof is leaky in rain, the library loses its dry status and can lose exposed paper stock (not mysteriously damage kitchen food). A source-linked inspection at the roof or floor gives a *qualitative* indication of condition. A repair action requires paid, delivered boards or roofing and an actual room visit; it changes floor/roof condition with physical receipts. Cold-room sleeping has reduced rest recovery and health costs. This is simplified tower physics, not a photograph of his floor plan.

**Garden neglect (E18-010):** After roughly a week of no tending, nettles increase slowly over days, reduce harvest yield and add physical fatigue while moving through the garden. Clearing the path requires an actual walk and task, recorded as `garden_tended`. No instantaneous punishment countdown.

**Epistemic boundary:** Mechanical weather and delivery logs are world events, but unseen remote consequences are marked `perceived:false` and may not become a personal memory. Operator access to exact stocks, dates and Swatch beats does not grant that knowledge to Septimus. His first-person perspective contains sensed cold, damp, seen objects and source-attested observations only. Some **legacy v0.1 behavior still reads global order status and supplies in its deterministic policy**, rather than a strict belief-state model. That known limit is not a claim of genuine first-person planning. The scene time seen on the operator interface is not an in-world clock for him.

**Diary feedback:** One retrospective page per day is favored after evening begins, only with a matching physical `journal_write` receipt. Selection favors distinct salient events over repeated eating, sleeping or cooking; deterministic templates bind source details such as object, count, counterparty, expense and damage. A folio-read receipt may elicit a response as a present reader of an old reconstructed account. Writing cannot silently rewrite verified events and does not leak ticks, beats or mechanical time into prose.

**Testing:** `tests/household.test.js` and `tests/household_diary.test.js` check seeded bounded transit, wet fuel, winter rest and health, roof leaks and repairs, drying state, stair damage and repairs, nettles after weeks, seasonal prices, physical diary provenance, and seeded seven-day page diversity. The retained standard `npm run experiment` tests 40 seeds for 700 ten-minute steps; the preregistered folio experiment is separate and must never advance the continuing world.

**Remaining limits:** Coal knowledge and old status checks are not yet fully gated by first-person observation; climate model is small and deterministic; rain cannot be seen outdoors visually beyond basic local senses; qualitative inspections are only a few coarse phrases; the historical archive is still tagged reconstructed and not observed in bulk; no animated villagers or delivery conversations are generated. These are stated limits, not passes.
