# Preregistered folio influence experiment v2 and winter observation

**Frozen before trials: November 1, 2026.** This is the protocol, not an outcome report. The original `docs/FOLIO_MEMORY_PREREGISTRATION.md` and `results/folio_experiment_v1.*` are immutable controls. No LLM, new cognitive organ, canonical rewrite, or living-world experiment.

## Epistemic and decision mechanism
A cue is a currently perceived **surface** tag, never a memory of the contents of a folio. Only actually perceived people, rooms, inspected or handled objects, sensed anomalies, and paid prices are eligible. Match their strings only to the existing `participants`, `locations`, and `recall_cues` metadata of the source-verified 450-record archive. No matching against `memory_text`, past claims, predicted goal, or privileged simulation variables. In particular, an unobserved delivery arrival is not a cue. Cues decay after 96 ticks (16 simulated hours); unresolved observations are exactly (cue-type, raised-tick) pairs that expire after 144 ticks (one day). A read of a matched folio clears only relevant flags. The reading receipt names the cue or chair trigger and selected folio index, source record is admitted only after a physical `journal_read` and verified `FOLIO_READ`. Selected-goal records must identify whether the relevant `FOLIO_READ` predates that decision. Unread folio text cannot enter choice.

The notebook is a rival candidate, not an obligation. Precommitted policy: when historical records are available, the candidate scores `35 + 0.9*boredom + max decayed matching-cue bonus + unresolved bonus + evening bonus`, with matching cue bonus initially 32 and linearly decaying to zero over 96 ticks; unresolved bonus 16 (at most one); evening bonus 16 during 18:00 to 23:00. The candidate is eligible at boredom >= 30 or with an active matched cue or unresolved flag. After a read, a 70-tick consultation cooldown prevents compulsive repeats. The chosen cause is `cue`, `question`, or `chair`, resolved in that order. A folio matched by an active cue wins over chronological/never-read browsing; otherwise choose the explicitly selected cursor (v1-compatible test preset) and then advance; autonomous browsing favors unread folios when possible. Read completion reduces boredom by 21, **measured** as before/after; do not tune it post-outcome. Ordinary survival crises retain their higher scores and interrupt travel.

## Matched controlled trial
Three unaltered v1 targets, E22-001 -> `inspect_floor`, E18-010 -> `tend_path`, E03-002 -> `order_coal`. Use 24 fixed seeds 1..24 **for each folio**, up to 800 ticks per pre-trigger run; repeat the same seed and full world setup across arms, never pick Septimus's goal externally. The v1 harness's only preset, a next-folio cursor, is retained. Record cues, goals, trigger cause, actual read index and whether a verified matching folio was accessed. A read of a different folio is not a target trigger; continue observing until the target or timeout. Record both all-folio and target-folio consultation; no-trigger seeds remain in all-seed denominators.

At the physical target-folio attempt clone the same post-trigger world into A, B, C. A: original verified source +18 target-goal policy effect; B: withheld (no folio content admitted, effect 0); C: harness-only clearly labeled `experiment_control_substitution` counter-folio (same v1 opposite claim, -18 effect); no control text enters `canon/`, ordinary saved worlds or character identity. The source digest, `FOLIO_READ` receipt for A/C (with disposable experimental access), and per-decision `postRead` evidence must be recorded. Intervention score is allowed only **after** the verified read, never at the pre-trigger chooser.

Follow each arm for 160 ticks. Primary outcome stays **first five subsequent `goal_selected` sequences A versus B**, across **all 24 seeds**, including no-trigger. Secondary outcomes: predicted action selected at least once in the window and A/C first-five divergence. The unchanged threshold is A/B difference >=30% of all seeds AND A target-selection rate > B; at least 2/3 folios must pass for `controller_score_influence`. Record arm trigger rate first, separated into cue/question/chair, then divergence. The recorded effect is engineered controller response, **not semantic comprehension**. If A/C act identically when reads occur, the read can be motivated without content-specific influence; if A/C differ this only establishes controller-mediated load-bearing content sign under this explicit intervention. Zero targets again means second null, not evidence of no possible effect. Report per-seed negative and positive cases unedited.

## Winter benchmark (measurement only)
Ten seeds 1..10, initial `World.fresh(seed)` resources, calendar shifted explicitly to **1899-11-01** (day 124 of July-1 origin); measure **60 full simulation days**, 144 ten-minute ticks/day, or death, without interventions or retuning. Collect wall-clock duration, run length, survival/death cause and tick, wallet at day start and each day boundary, coal-empty episodes and total empty ticks, library dry-room true->false losses and false->true recoveries, repair counts, and full-life indicator defined without change: witnessed `drink_sink`, `eat_meal`, `cook`, `harvest`, `catalog_order`, `collect_parcel`, `read_section`, `experiment`. Preserve all seeds including premature deaths. No changes to household parameters, climate or prices are allowed after protocol freeze. This benchmark is evidence, not a launch gate.
