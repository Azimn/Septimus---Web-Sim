# Septimus Tower: designated successor to the standalone Pretorius Laboratory

**Decision date:** 2026-11-02  
**Status:** Web-Sim is the chosen successor now. Selective feature recovery remains in progress. **Parity with the failed old Laboratory is neither required nor desired.**  
**Source heads reviewed:** Septimus Web-Sim `b70a53faa47b5c52d4b2f540611aef74320b22b9`; Pretorius-Laboratory `6e49795752835d64fa6fd63d16359669a1ca2ba7`; The-Doctor-Lives main at the November 2, 2026 inspection.  
**Scope:** The physical/life simulator only. The Doctor Lives remains Pretorius's independent authoritative cognitive system.

## Executive architectural decision

Septimus Web-Sim is promoted from disposable research toy to the **target home-world simulation** for Pretorius's pre-Village ruined tower outside Ingolstadt. The old Python/SQLite Pretorius-Laboratory is a **read-only reference and evidence archive** for selected mechanical ideas, not a second active world, an executable dependency or a program whose complexity must be recreated. Its repeated failures as a functioning whole-house simulation are the reason for this pivot. Its immutable history is retained. The Doctor Lives remains authoritative for identity, active cognition, autobiographical memory, relationships, concerns, neural policy, commitments and ongoing subjective continuity.

**Designation is not a cutover.** The existing Web-Sim deterministic `Simulation.choose` is a useful validated baseline household controller, not Pretorius's complete cognitive policy. His local `identity.json`, 450 reconstructed folios and small rule-based `knowledge.facts` must not silently overwrite The Doctor Lives' schema, 70-node Persona Connectome, deep-history classifications or living memory. A successful migration must contain **one physical world authority, one cognitive authority, and an explicit checked bridge**.

The two modeled buildings are not proven to be the same actual location. Web-Sim's scenario is the **ruined tower, occupied since 1868**, beginning July 1899. Pretorius-Laboratory's opt-in home prototype has a **sanctum, experimental chamber, bedroom, kitchen, sitting room and closed curiosity shop** associated with a *future* Village-facing story. That shop's curtain, inventory, events and arrival are **not automatically present** in the tower. Recover a few validated behaviors through new Web-Sim-native implementations, not the older simulator's internal architecture, foreign save format, invented historical possessions or movement.

## Explicit correction: selective salvage, not parity

**Reason for the pivot:** The old Laboratory repeatedly failed to function as the intended autonomous inhabited home. Its individually implemented fixtures and unit tests are useful evidence that *some mechanisms can work*, but they never established that the whole system could sustain a credible life. Recreating its multiple overlapping Python/SQLite domains, subject ports, ledgers, migrations and experimental host scaffolding would reverse the successful simplification that produced Web-Sim V5.

**Nonnegotiable integration rules:** Web-Sim remains one Node.js simulation with one authoritative world timeline, one native action/goal loop, and one native persistent save history. Its own baseline must continue to launch and run with **no Pretorius-Laboratory Python package, second SQLite authority, external model, cross-repository runtime, or mandatory network call**. Source assets such as public-domain books are data, not execution dependencies. A foreign Laboratory save is not a migration requirement because it never represented the same tower or an established past there.

Recover **outcomes** rather than code structure: actual book text and bookmarks, object identity/one-location custody, bounded experimental measurements, darkness/local physical constraints, and task duration. Implement each as a small module attached to the current `world`, `organism` and `simulation` primitives; do not import the earlier experimental architecture or duplicate location, perception or receipt authorities.

**Fitness function:** A salvaged mechanic is retained only if it adds a meaningful action or falsifiable environmental constraint, preserves existing save compatibility, passes focused regression and adverse-condition tests, and survives full unattended household runs without degrading the life loop. If it breaks winter survivability, produces significant idle travel, stalls research income, or increases complexity without observable behavior, simplify or remove that change rather than building adapters around a failing subsystem. Existing V5 results remain frozen controls, not success claims for new rules.

**Build strategy:** Preserve V5 unchanged as a golden baseline; use isolated experimental branches; make one thin vertical slice at a time; compare with baseline after each feature; then merge only with reproducible evidence. The first slice is a **single actual text-backed book, local reading/custody and bookmark persistence**, followed by the remaining verified corpus. Do not demand all seventeen books before testing the first. Next add a single bounded instrument/measurement and incremental duration model. Keep the actual Doctor Lives cognitive bridge a **separate opt-in research track**, not a prerequisite to trusting or shipping the tower's autonomous physical simulation.

## P0 successor gates, in dependency order

### A. Versioned successor contract and current-save safety

Freeze a native Web-Sim source/epoch/clock compatibility table and stable IDs for the existing tower coordinates, room names, journals, catalog orders, receipts, garden, roof/floor condition and possessions. The existing saved `events.jsonl` must verify unchanged. Add a **native versioned state extension** that preserves earlier events, rather than editing genesis, importing old Laboratory SQLite world history, inventing a prior book purchase, rewriting historical folios, or silently merging the old Laboratory's shop. Validate full backup, restore, duplicate receipt, stale revision, interrupted mutation, and a separate experimental fork. A single physical event has exactly one authoritative owner.

**Acceptance:** existing Septimus saves reopen; original hashes pass; tampering is detected; restart/retry produces no duplicated food, objects, income, memory, or post receipts.

### B. Multi-tick activities and believable daily rhythm

Implement bounded declared activity durations and completed-action effects for sleep, experiments, repair, cooking, reading, instrument measurements, carrying, cleaning and letter writing. Unlike current one-tick `sleep`, rest takes actual time; hunger, thirst, room temperature and fuel continue evolving. Persist activity phase, object reservation, partial progress and interrupted work. Make travel, study and sleep time economically and physiologically comparable. Distinguish `rest` and sustained `sleep`, and prohibit learning/research rewards before completion. Re-run the **same twenty seeds**, including negative seeds, with no artificial wallet, refills or economic retuning. The 20/20 v5 result must remain historical, never falsely labeled as proof under the new duration rules.

**Acceptance:** any interrupted activity resumes or explicitly fails without duplication; sleep duration matches world time and is not an instant heal; real future task completion can still support at least one healthy 60-day life before seeking a robust cohort.

### C. Source-backed library and physical custody

Adapt the two pinned public-domain book corpora into a new small native tower library, without treating modern transcriptions as authenticated historical printings. Begin with **one** verified actual book, physically carried to a reading position and bookmarkable after restart; then incrementally add all **17 source-backed books** with stable positions, checked editions, readable text offsets, custody/placement history and source text. Keep the old eight fictional placeholder titles only as explicitly non-source-backed scenario props or remove them via a versioned migration. A verified `read_section` cannot grant a whole book's contents; reading must operate over actual installed text. Folio reading and The Doctor Lives' historical memories remain separate evidence classes. Support reading in place, carrying a book, putting it down, resuming at a bookmark, and an unavailable/dark room.

**Acceptance per slice:** the first book has real verified text, persists across restart, cannot exist in two places, and cannot be read while absent; prove those invariants before expanding to 17/17 complete verified sources. No invented pages, fabricated learning or changing reading provenance.

### D. Subject viewpoint and actual Pretorius brain

As a separate optional integration after the stand-alone tower is stable, implement a narrow cross-process adapter from new world receipts to `The-Doctor-Lives.PretoriusBrainPort`, reusing the strict first-person projection and typed source-authority boundaries. Admit **seen** world events only, never operator inventories, hidden economic state, arbitrary engine JSON or unseen mail arrivals. Idempotently map an accepted world receipt to exactly one lived-memory admission. Provide a cognitive request/evaluation surface and an explicitly auditable authorization path for the resulting **world act**. Preserve Web-Sim's deterministic policy as a **matched baseline and safe fallback**, not a second personality. Do not count shared natural-language perception or a host-started activity queue as evidence that the real Doctor autonomously selected it. Do not let an LLM write directly to world inventory or historical canon.

**Acceptance for the optional brain track:** memory-withheld, stale-observation, false-claim and restart/duplicate controls pass; real-Doctor proposals and matched simple-controller proposals use equal current observations and action affordances; causal differences are recorded from executed, receipted outcomes. Failure of this track must not prevent stand-alone Web-Sim from operating normally.

### E. Visible condition and viable choices

Add the old Laboratory's light/room-local perception gates, finite lamp use and scene-local physical access; introduce a persistent general object layer with container capacity, carrying, weight/size/reach, and measured rather than omniscient knowledge. Maintain a subject-only natural-language presentation and an engineer-only numeric audit. Expand options through **affordances**, not by giving an arbitrary free-action menu. A room and its actual objects determine legal choices. Reuse the old fixed synthetic ruler/balance as a declared engineering scenario, not authentic Victorian measurements. Preserve the existing porch mail/catalog lifecycle and roof, fuel and garden simulation.

**Acceptance:** dark or distant items cannot be read, measured, fetched or enumerated; physically occupied slots reject a second occupant; instrument measurements produce reproducible but uncertain source receipts; bad decisions can cause genuine reversible costs and recognized failure.

## P1: make the tower worth inhabiting

The meaningful missing *choices* are not more fake currencies or off-map errands. Give the same room richer opportunity: choosing, opening and using books; writing annotated research observations; preparing different meals from actual ingredients; tending and cultivating specific plants, pressing flowers, drying herbs and keeping records; lighting and maintaining apparatus; collecting and storing specimens; running controlled measurements; cleaning/repairing furniture; maintaining, inspecting and organizing archives; sorting correspondence and sending non-income letters; and quiet leisure, reflection or reading by choice. Every option needs a local object, resources, consequences, elapsed time, and source-grounded receipts. Distinguish character goals from the host's engineering experiment harness.

Prioritize a **small vertical slice** over adding every noun at once: one real book with portable custody, one measured apparatus, one multi-stage investigation, one domestic recovery loop, one correspondence thread and one interrupted-return-to-task test. Expand to the full seventeen books and the remaining interactions only after the vertical slice is reproducible.

## P2: scientific quality and release gates

After each accepted native capability slice, test a continuous July-to-December life from ordinary July initial conditions without a separate November infusion. Preserve daily need/cash/travel/coal/resource traces, not just whether Septimus is alive. Use matched challenging seeds, bad-policy and withheld-evidence controls to make failure meaningful. Include 60-day warmth/health, house wear, book study, experiments, self-selected tasks, and source-driven belief revision. Run cross-restart and cross-model continuity tests; the actual Doctor and the simple controller must be distinguishable in a predeclared, held-out outcome, or report the null.

Science should have falsifiable inputs, apparatus, observations, failure modes, reusable notes and follow-up investigation. `experiment` should not always be an instant guaranteed report and payment. Model sustainable economics without hardcoded forced poverty. Historical sources, synthetic scenario facts, and actually lived receipts remain separate. The future Village shop/arrival is a **later independent story and integration gate**, not an ordinary household feature.

## Ownership and promotion semantics

**NEW PHYSICAL OWNER (designated):** `Septimus---Web-Sim` for the ruined tower, its autonomous household, local objects, material consequences and virtual time.

**OLD MECHANICS REFERENCE (kept intact):** `Pretorius-Laboratory` supplies selected public-domain data, verified research notes, isolated mechanical examples and preserved negative controls. No runtime imports, architectural parity gate, mirrored live world, old save rewrite, or tower=shop assertion.

**EXISTING COGNITIVE OWNER (unchanged):** `The-Doctor-Lives` for Pretorius's active identity/memory/cognition/relationships. The Web-Sim's deterministic chooser is a temporary controlled mode until an explicit bridge and matched scientific validation exists.

**Handoff condition:** Web-Sim is **already the chosen primary house simulation**; the failed Laboratory does not remain a production baseline that Web-Sim must imitate. Its old tests and historical experiments remain available as immutable research controls. Web-Sim may ship useful new native affordances once they pass autonomous stability, save integrity and physical truthfulness gates, independently of the optional Doctor brain integration. Do not delete historical experiments or alter Pretorius identity.

**Decision metric:** Does Septimus maintain a life while producing a growing, trustworthy history of meaningful actions, choices, knowledge and changed surroundings? Mere 60-day survival and large wallet balances are necessary evidence for viability, not the complete game.
