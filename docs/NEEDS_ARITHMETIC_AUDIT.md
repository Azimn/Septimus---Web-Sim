## Limitations and definitions
A simulated day is **144 steps of 10 minutes**. Every action completes on one tick **only after** travel to its mapped object at one grid square per tick; an action cycle is `pathBetween(current position, object).length + 1` ticks. A goal may be interrupted by critical needs, and a scientific goal can require multiple work ticks to light the lamp and clear the bench. A source-level rate calculation is a bound, not proof that a resource-constrained planner can attain it.

`warmth` is **not** a numerical meter with accrual. It is a derived room comfort score; `health` has damage but no general replenishing action. The previous ten winter deaths all had sampled warmth **25.0** because the final room was unheated, dry, and in the cold season: `25 season base + 0 pipe boost + 0 dryness penalty + 0 roof penalty`, as computed by `Household.comfort()`. It is not evidence of a stored `warmth=25` state or proof that each died of hypothermia. Verify this interpretation using the final room and physical heat status in the replay.
