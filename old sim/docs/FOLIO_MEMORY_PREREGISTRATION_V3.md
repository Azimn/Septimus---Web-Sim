## Epistemic contract
Perceived world *surfaces* may cue lookup using only `participants`, `locations` and `recall_cues`; these are not knowledge. A real folio must be physically read, then have a source-digest-checked `FOLIO_READ` ledger entry, before any corresponding `BELIEF_GRANT` may be appended. For in-world living saves, store grants append-only with source folio, verified read ID, grant tick, fact ID and literal source-interpretation label. On reload verify chain and associated receipt, source hash, fact allowlist and temporal order; reject forged grants. A perceived direct-encounter receipt may grant the same positive fact, with source kind `experienced` and real receipt ID, but never for `perceived:false`. False facts are harness-only with `experiment_control_substitution` provenance; never persist to lived saves. Planner must cite every held fact used, including source type and grant tick, in each `goal_selected` and candidate record. A future grant may not affect earlier decisions. Reconstructed history remains distinct from experienced life.

Allowlist for hand-declared positive facts: E22-001 -> `floor_east_rotten`, `floor_inspection_warranted`; E03-002 -> `coal_unreliable`; E18-010 -> `paths_nettle_untended`. Explicit harness-only counter-claims: E22-001 -> `floor_sound`; E03-002 -> `coal_reliable`; E18-010 -> `paths_clear`. Ordinary experience can grant floor concern from sensed flex/hazard, coal risk from a collected wet delivery or an actually felt stockout, and nettle risk from a visually encountered overgrown path. False claims alter behavior but do not alter physical physics. No arbitrary text matching or inference on unread records. Knowledge affects concrete planner scoring: unsafe-floor inspection/repair and avoidance of carrying heavy parcels over damaged stairs; advance coal reserve purchase; preemptive garden tending. Acute survival needs still win. Never claim this is semantic LLM comprehension: all interpretation is hand-coded.
